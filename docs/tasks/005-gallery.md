# Task 005: Visual Photo & Video Gallery Page

## Objective
Construct the photo and video showcase page (`src/app/(public)/gallery/page.tsx`) displaying high-resolution culinary, cocktail, event, and interior ambience photography.

---

## Action Items

- [ ] Create Category Filter Bar (All, Food, Cocktails, Interior Ambience, Private Events).

---

## Deliverables & Acceptance Criteria

- Full-screen Lightbox modal fully keyboard accessible.
